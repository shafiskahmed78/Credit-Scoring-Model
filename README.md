# ****Credit-Scoring-Model****
# **Predicting Credit Card Default Risks**
## **Defining Credit Scoring**
Credit scoring: A statistical analysis used by lenders to assess the creditworthiness of credit card applicants.
### **Significance in Financial Decision-Making**
Crucial for financial institutions to make informed lending decisions.
Helps in assessing the risk of lending and mitigating potential losses.
#### **Impact on Economy and Consumers**
Affects the overall health of the financial system and individual access to credit.
Influences interest rates, credit limits, and loan approvals.
##### **Predictive Power of Data**
Utilizing historical data to predict future behavior.
Involves analyzing past credit history, payment patterns, and socio-economic factors.
###### **Project Focus**
Aiming to predict the likelihood of customers defaulting on credit card payments.
Leveraging machine learning to identify key predictors of default.
